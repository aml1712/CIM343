AFTERGLOW

Afterglow is a three day music festival in the heart of San Diego, California, bringing together live music, unforgettable performances, and the vibrant energy of the West Coast! From rising artists to big name headliners, Afterglow is all about discovering new sounds, dancing with friends, and making memories that last long after the sun goes down! With the San Diego skyline, warm nights, and ocean breeze as our backdrop, Afterglow is where the music, the people, and the night come alive!
