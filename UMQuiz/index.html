  • Question 1: When was the University of Miami founded?

    The University of Miami was founded in 1925! It was envisioned as both a beacon of higher education and a cornerstone of Coral Gables by founder George Merrick!

  • Qustion 2: What is the Univeristy of Miami's mascot?

    The Icon, The Myth, The Legend: SEBASTIAN THE IBIS! Folklore maintains that the Ibis, a symbol of knowledge found in the Everglades & Egypt, is the last sign of wildlife to take shelter before a hurricane and the first to reappear after the storm!

  • Question 3: What are the Univeristy of Miami's school colors?

    The official University of Miami colors are ORANGE and GREEN! The two colors represent the Florida orange tree; Orange symbolizes the fruit of the tree, green represents the leaves and white, the blossoms.
